Can the police arrest you without a warrant? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ Yes, in cases the law lists, like a cognizable offence committed in front of them
2️⃣ Accused (not caught in the act) of a cognizable offence with up to 7 years' jail? Arrest must be necessary, with reasons in writing
3️⃣ If it isn't, you get a notice to appear. Comply, and you shall not be arrested unless police record why
4️⃣ Infirm or over 60, offence under 3 years? Police need prior permission from a DSP or above
5️⃣ If arrested, you must be told the grounds, and someone you name must be informed

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 35, 47, 48
