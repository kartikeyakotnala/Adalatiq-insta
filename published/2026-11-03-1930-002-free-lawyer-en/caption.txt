Can't afford a lawyer? You may be entitled to one for free. 👇

Under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, these people get free legal services:
• Every woman and child
• SC/ST members
• People with a disability or mental illness
• Industrial workers
• Anyone in custody
• Victims of trafficking, disasters, ethnic violence or caste atrocities
• People with income below their State's limit

It covers a lawyer, court fees and case expenses.
📞 Call NALSA's free helpline: 15100

Sources: Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Sec 12); nalsa.gov.in
