Need information from a government office? Use your Right to Information. 👇

1️⃣ Every citizen has the right to information under the RTI Act
2️⃣ Apply in writing or online, in English, Hindi or the local official language. No reason needed
3️⃣ Fee: ₹10 for central government offices (states set their own). No fee for BPL applicants (with a BPL certificate)
4️⃣ Reply due in 30 days, or 48 hours if life or liberty is involved
5️⃣ Late reply? The information is free
6️⃣ First appeal within 30 days, second appeal to the Information Commission within 90 days

Source: Right to Information Act, 2005 (Sec 3, 6, 7, 19); RTI Rules, 2012
