Someone's cheque bounced on you? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ A cheque bouncing for lack of funds can be a criminal offence
2️⃣ Present it within its validity: 3 months from its date
3️⃣ Send a written notice within 30 days of the bank telling you
4️⃣ No payment within 15 days? File a complaint within the next month
5️⃣ Penalty: up to 2 years' jail and/or a fine up to twice the cheque amount

Source: Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (Sec 138, 142, 143A); RBI notification (Nov 2011)
