Do daughters have a share in joint family (coparcenary) property? Yes. Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ A daughter becomes a coparcener by birth, just like a son
2️⃣ Same rights and same liabilities in joint family property
3️⃣ It applies even if the father died before 9 September 2005; her rights run from that date (Supreme Court, 2020)
4️⃣ Her share equals a son's share
5️⃣ Exception: partitions, sales, gifts or wills made before 20 December 2004 stand
⚠️ This is about coparcenary property. A father's self-acquired property is not a birthright. He can will it; if he dies without a will, sons and daughters inherit equal shares as Class I heirs, along with his widow and mother.

Source: Hindu Succession Act, 1956 (Sec 2, 6, 8, 10, 30); Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma (2020)
