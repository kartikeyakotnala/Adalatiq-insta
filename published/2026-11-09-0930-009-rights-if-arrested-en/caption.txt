Arrested? These are the rights the law gives you. 👇

1️⃣ Arrested without a warrant? Police must tell you the full grounds of arrest at once
2️⃣ Not accused of a non-bailable offence? Police must also tell you you're entitled to bail
3️⃣ A relative, friend or person you name must be told about the arrest and where you're held
4️⃣ You can meet an advocate of your choice during questioning (not for all of it)
5️⃣ A medical check soon after arrest, with a copy of the report
6️⃣ You must be taken before a Magistrate. No more than 24 hours in police custody without a Magistrate's order (travel time excluded)

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 38, 47, 48, 53, 57, 58; Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 – Section 12; NALSA
