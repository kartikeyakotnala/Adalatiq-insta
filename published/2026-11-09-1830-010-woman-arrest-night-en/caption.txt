Can the police arrest a woman at night? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ Except in exceptional circumstances, no woman shall be arrested between sunset and sunrise
2️⃣ In an exceptional case, a woman police officer must make a written report and get a first-class Magistrate's prior permission
3️⃣ Unless circumstances require it, a male officer shall not touch a woman to arrest her
4️⃣ A woman can only be searched by another woman, with strict regard to decency
5️⃣ Her medical examination must be by, or supervised by, a woman doctor

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 38, 43, 47, 48, 49, 51, 53; Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 – Section 12
