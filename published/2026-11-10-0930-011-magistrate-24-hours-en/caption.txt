How long can the police keep you before a Magistrate? 👇

1️⃣ Without a Magistrate's special order, no more than 24 hours
2️⃣ Travel time from the place of arrest to the court isn't counted
3️⃣ Police must take you before a Magistrate without unnecessary delay
4️⃣ The Magistrate can first order custody for up to 15 days (in whole or in parts); longer detention needs adequate grounds
5️⃣ Total detention during investigation: 90 days if the offence is punishable with death, life or 10+ years, 60 days for others. After that, bail if you're ready to furnish it

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 57, 58, 187
