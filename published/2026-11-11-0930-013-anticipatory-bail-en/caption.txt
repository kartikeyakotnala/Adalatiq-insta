Worried you may be arrested? Here's how anticipatory bail works under the new law. 👇

1️⃣ If you fear arrest for a non-bailable offence, you may apply to the High Court or the Court of Session
2️⃣ If the court thinks fit, it can direct that you be released on bail if arrested
3️⃣ The court can add conditions, like being available for police interrogation and not leaving India without permission
4️⃣ If arrested later on that accusation and you're ready to give bail, you shall be released on bail
5️⃣ It doesn't apply to accusations under BNS Sec 65 or Sec 70(2) (certain rape cases involving minors)

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Section 482; Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 65, 70(2)
