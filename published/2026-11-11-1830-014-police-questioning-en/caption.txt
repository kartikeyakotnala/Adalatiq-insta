Police called you in for questioning? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ As a witness, a male under 15 or above 60, a woman, or a disabled or acutely ill person can't be required to attend anywhere other than where they live
2️⃣ They can still choose to go to the police station
3️⃣ Suspected of a cognizable offence and arrest isn't needed? Police must give you a notice to appear, and complying is your duty
4️⃣ Comply, and you shall not be arrested unless police record reasons. Ignore it, and you can be arrested
5️⃣ No person accused of an offence can be compelled to be a witness against himself

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 35, 179; Constitution of India – Article 20(3); MHA
