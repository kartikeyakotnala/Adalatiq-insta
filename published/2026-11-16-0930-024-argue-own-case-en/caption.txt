Can't afford a lawyer? Here's what the law says about arguing your own case. 👇

1️⃣ In a civil case, you can appear and act in court in person
2️⃣ The court can still direct you to appear personally
3️⃣ Only advocates practise law, but a court may permit a non-advocate in a particular case
4️⃣ An accused person has the right to be defended by an advocate of their choice
5️⃣ If an accused can't afford one, the court must assign an advocate at the State's expense
6️⃣ Free legal services are available to women, children, SC/ST members, persons in custody and others

Source: CPC, 1908 (Order III Rule 1); Advocates Act, 1961 (Sec 29, 32, 33); BNSS, 2023 (Sec 340, 341); Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Sec 12)
