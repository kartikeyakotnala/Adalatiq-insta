Got a legal notice? What it means depends on the law behind it. 👇

1️⃣ Before suing the Government or a public officer over an official act, a written notice and a two-month wait are required
2️⃣ That notice must state the cause of action, your details and the relief claimed
3️⃣ Bounced cheque: the payee must send a written demand within 30 days of hearing from the bank
4️⃣ The drawer then has 15 days to pay. If not, it can be an offence with up to 2 years' jail, a fine up to twice the cheque amount, or both
5️⃣ A police notice to appear must be complied with. Not complying can lead to arrest
6️⃣ Whether and how to reply depends on the notice, so get legal advice quickly

Source: CPC, 1908 (Sec 80); Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (Sec 138, 142); BNSS, 2023 (Sec 35)
