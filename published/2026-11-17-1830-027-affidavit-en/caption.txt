Asked to submit an affidavit? Here's what it is. 👇

1️⃣ An affidavit is a statement of facts you swear or affirm before an authorised person
2️⃣ The oath can be given by a court or Magistrate, a notary, or a Commissioner of Oaths appointed by a court
3️⃣ Stick to facts you know yourself. State any belief separately, with its grounds
4️⃣ In civil cases, a witness's examination-in-chief is on affidavit
5️⃣ The court can call the deponent for cross-examination
6️⃣ The Centre has asked Ministries and State Governments to allow self-certification in place of affidavits

Source: CPC, 1908 (Sec 139; Order XVIII Rule 4; Order XIX); BNSS, 2023 (Sec 332, 333); Notaries Act, 1952 (Sec 8); PMO
