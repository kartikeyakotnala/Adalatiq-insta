Shop refusing to refund a faulty product? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ A defect is any fault or shortcoming in quality, quantity, purity or standard
2️⃣ Refusing to take back defective goods and refund within the period in the bill (or 30 days if none) is an unfair trade practice
3️⃣ Not giving a bill or cash memo is also an unfair trade practice
4️⃣ Try the National Consumer Helpline first: call 1915 or WhatsApp 8800001915
5️⃣ Still stuck? File a consumer complaint, online on e-Jagriti
6️⃣ The Commission can order removal of the defect, replacement, a refund with interest, or compensation

Source: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sec 2(6), 2(10), 2(47), 38(7), 39); Dept of Consumer Affairs; PIB
