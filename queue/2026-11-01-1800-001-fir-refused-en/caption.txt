Police refused to register your FIR? Here's what the law says you can do. 👇

1️⃣ Any police station must record a cognizable offence, wherever it happened (Zero FIR)
2️⃣ You can report it electronically, then sign within 3 days
3️⃣ You get a free copy of what's recorded
4️⃣ Refused? Write to the Superintendent of Police by post
5️⃣ SP doesn't act? Apply to the Magistrate

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Section 173
