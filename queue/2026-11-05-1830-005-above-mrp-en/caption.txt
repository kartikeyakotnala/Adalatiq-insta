Charged more than the MRP? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ The MRP is the maximum price a packaged product can be sold for
2️⃣ It already includes all taxes, so no GST can be added on top
3️⃣ No seller may charge more than the printed retail sale price
4️⃣ Complain to the National Consumer Helpline: call 1915, or WhatsApp/SMS 8800001915

Source: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011; PIB (Nov 2021); consumerhelpline.gov.in
