Got a faulty product or bad service? Here's how to file a consumer complaint. 👇

1️⃣ File online on e-Daakhil (edaakhil.nic.in), available across all States/UTs
2️⃣ At the District or State Commission, you can file where you live or work
3️⃣ Up to ₹50 lakh: District Commission. ₹50 lakh to ₹2 crore: State. Above ₹2 crore: National
4️⃣ No filing fee if you paid up to ₹5 lakh
5️⃣ File within 2 years from when the cause of action arises

Source: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sec 2(7), 34, 35, 69); Jurisdiction Rules 2021; CDRC Rules 2020 (as amended 2023); PIB
