Bailable or non-bailable? Here's the difference. 👇

1️⃣ Bailable means the BNSS First Schedule (or another law) says so. All other offences are non-bailable
2️⃣ In bailable offences, bail is the right of the accused, and police can grant it too
3️⃣ Arrested without a warrant for a bailable offence? Police must tell you that you're entitled to bail
4️⃣ Non-bailable doesn't mean no bail. It's at the discretion of the court or police officer
5️⃣ Examples: hurt and rash driving are bailable. Murder and rape are non-bailable

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – Sections 2(1)(c), 47(2), 478, 480, First Schedule
