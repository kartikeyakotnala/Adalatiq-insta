Crime happened somewhere else? You can still go to the nearest police station. 👇

1️⃣ Information about a cognizable offence can be given to any police station, wherever the offence happened (Zero FIR)
2️⃣ You can give it orally or by electronic communication
3️⃣ Reported electronically? Sign it within 3 days so it is taken on record
4️⃣ You get a copy of the FIR free of cost
5️⃣ How to report online depends on your State's police. For cyber financial fraud, use the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal or call 1930
