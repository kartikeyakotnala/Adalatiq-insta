Lost money to an online scam? Here's how to report it. 👇

1️⃣ Call the toll-free helpline 1930 or report on cybercrime.gov.in
2️⃣ Report financial fraud immediately: the system exists to stop fraudsters siphoning off funds
3️⃣ Tell your bank at the earliest. Under RBI rules, loss after you report is borne by the bank
4️⃣ Bank's negligence or deficiency? Zero liability, and the bank must prove any liability is yours
5️⃣ Only got a suspicious call, SMS or WhatsApp? Report it on Chakshu (sancharsaathi.gov.in). For money lost, use 1930
