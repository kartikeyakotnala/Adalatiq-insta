Being threatened online? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ Threatening someone by any means with injury to person, reputation or property can be criminal intimidation
2️⃣ Punishment: up to 2 years, or fine, or both. Threats to kill or cause grievous hurt: up to 7 years
3️⃣ A man monitoring a woman's internet, email or electronic communication commits stalking
4️⃣ Save all communications as evidence, then report and block
5️⃣ Report on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
6️⃣ Information about a cognizable offence can be given to police by electronic communication; sign it within 3 days

Source: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 78, 351); Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 173); I4C, MHA
