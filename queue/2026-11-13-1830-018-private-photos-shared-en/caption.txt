Someone shared your private photos online? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ Complain to the platform: it must take all reasonable steps to remove or disable such content within 2 hours
2️⃣ Use the platform's Grievance Officer. Not satisfied? Appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee within 30 days
3️⃣ Report on cybercrime.gov.in, call 1930, or go to your local police station
4️⃣ Capturing or sharing an image of someone's private area without consent, violating their privacy: up to 3 years, or fine up to ₹2 lakh, or both
5️⃣ A woman's consent to an image of a private act being captured is not consent to share it. Sharing it is voyeurism under BNS Sec 77
