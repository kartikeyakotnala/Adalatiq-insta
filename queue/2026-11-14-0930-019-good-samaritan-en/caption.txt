Scared to help at a road accident? The law is on your side. 👇

1️⃣ Helping an accident victim in good faith? You're protected from civil or criminal liability
2️⃣ No one can force you to give your name, address or other personal details
3️⃣ Being a witness is your choice. If you volunteer, you may be examined once, at a time and place convenient to you
4️⃣ Hospitals cannot demand payment from you for emergency care, and you're free to leave once the patient is admitted
5️⃣ Help a serious road accident victim get medical care within the Golden Hour and you can be eligible for a ₹25,000 Rah-Veer reward

Source: Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (Sec 134A); Good Samaritan Rules, 2020 (GSR 594(E)); PIB (MoRTH)
