Lent money and not getting it back? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ Not repaying is usually a civil matter, not cheating
2️⃣ It can be cheating only if there was a fraudulent or dishonest intention right from the beginning
3️⃣ Your remedy is a civil suit to recover the money. The criminal process can't be used just to force payment
4️⃣ For a suit for money lent, you usually get 3 years from when the loan is made
5️⃣ A signed acknowledgment or a part-payment (acknowledged in writing) before time runs out starts a fresh period

Source: Supreme Court (2024 INSC 626; 2026 INSC 846); Limitation Act, 1963 (Sec 3, 18, 19; Schedule Art 19, 21)
