Stuck in a long court case? A Lok Adalat may help you settle it. 👇

1️⃣ It helps parties reach a compromise, for pending cases and disputes not yet filed in court
2️⃣ No court fee is payable, and if a case referred from court is settled, the court fee is refunded
3️⃣ The award is treated as a civil court decree. It's final and binding, with no appeal
4️⃣ No settlement? The case goes back to the court and continues from where it was
5️⃣ It can't deal with offences that are not compoundable
6️⃣ National Lok Adalats are held on a single day in courts across the country

Source: Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Sec 19, 20, 21, 22A, 22C); NALSA
