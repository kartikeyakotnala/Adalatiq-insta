Want to track your court case from home? Here's how. 👇

1️⃣ District/Taluka court case: search on services.ecourts.gov.in by case number, party name, advocate or FIR number
2️⃣ Have your 16-character CNR number? Enter it without hyphens or spaces
3️⃣ The free eCourts Services app covers High Courts and District Courts. Save cases under "My Cases"
4️⃣ No internet? SMS ECOURTS <space> your CNR number to 9766899899
5️⃣ Supreme Court case: check the case status page on sci.gov.in
