Thinking of going to court? Check the deadline first. 👇

1️⃣ A case filed after the limitation period shall be dismissed, even if the other side doesn't object
2️⃣ Money lent or breach of contract: 3 years. Possession of immovable property based on title: 12 years
3️⃣ Consumer complaint: 2 years from the cause of action
4️⃣ Civil appeal: 90 days to a High Court, 30 days to any other court
5️⃣ A signed written acknowledgment of liability, made in time, starts a fresh period
6️⃣ Late appeals and applications may be admitted if you show sufficient cause

Source: Limitation Act, 1963 (Sec 3, 4, 5, 12, 18; Schedule Art 19, 55, 65, 116, 136); Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sec 69)
