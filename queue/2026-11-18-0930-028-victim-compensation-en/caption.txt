Hurt in a crime? You may be able to get compensation from your State's victim compensation scheme. 👇

1️⃣ Every State, with the Centre, must run a scheme to compensate crime victims or their dependants who need rehabilitation
2️⃣ The DLSA or SLSA decides the amount, so it depends on your State's scheme
3️⃣ The court can recommend compensation, even if the case ends in acquittal or discharge
4️⃣ Offender not traced or identified, but the victim is identified, and no trial? You can apply directly to the SLSA or DLSA
5️⃣ The enquiry must be completed within 2 months, and urgent free medical help or interim relief can be ordered
6️⃣ It is in addition to fines paid to the victim under certain BNS sections (rape, gang rape, acid attack)

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 396, 397); NALSA Compensation Scheme for Women Victims, 2018
