Paid online but your order never came? Here's what the rules say. 👇

1️⃣ Online purchases are covered by the Consumer Protection Act and the E-Commerce Rules, 2020
2️⃣ A seller or platform can't refuse a refund if delivery is late from the stated schedule (except force majeure)
3️⃣ Complain to the platform's grievance officer: acknowledgement within 48 hours, redressal within 1 month
4️⃣ Marketplaces must give you a ticket number to track your complaint
5️⃣ No luck? Call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or WhatsApp 8800001915
6️⃣ Still unresolved? File a consumer complaint, online on e-Jagriti

Source: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (Rules 2, 4, 5, 6, 7); Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sec 2(6), 2(7)); PIB
