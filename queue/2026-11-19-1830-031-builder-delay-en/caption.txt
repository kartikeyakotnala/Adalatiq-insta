Builder delayed your flat? RERA gives you clear rights. 👇

1️⃣ Delay in possession as per the agreement? You can withdraw or stay on
2️⃣ Withdraw: the builder must refund what it received, with interest, plus compensation
3️⃣ Stay on: the builder must pay interest for every month of delay till possession
4️⃣ The interest rate is set by rules, so it depends on your State
5️⃣ Complain to your State's Real Estate Regulatory Authority or its adjudicating officer
6️⃣ Unhappy with the order? Appeal to the Appellate Tribunal within 60 days

Source: Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (Sec 18, 19, 31, 44, 71, 72)
