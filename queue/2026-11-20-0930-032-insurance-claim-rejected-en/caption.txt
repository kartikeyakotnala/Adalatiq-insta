Insurance claim rejected? You don't have to accept it quietly. 👇

1️⃣ First, complain to your insurer in writing, with documents
2️⃣ Not resolved in two weeks? Escalate to IRDAI on Bima Bharosa, by email or on 155255
3️⃣ The Insurance Ombudsman hears complaints on claim rejection, claim delay and premium disputes
4️⃣ Go to the Ombudsman if the insurer rejects you, doesn't reply in a month, or you're not satisfied, within one year
5️⃣ File online on cioins.co.in or by email
6️⃣ The award can give up to Rs 50 lakh and is binding on the insurer

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (Rules 13, 14, 17); IRDAI
