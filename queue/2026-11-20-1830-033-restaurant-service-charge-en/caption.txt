Service charge on your restaurant bill? You don't have to pay it. 👇

1️⃣ No restaurant can force you to pay a service charge. It is voluntary and optional
2️⃣ It can't be added to the bill automatically or by default
3️⃣ It can't be collected under any other name
4️⃣ You can't be refused entry or service for not paying it
5️⃣ In March 2025, the Delhi High Court upheld the CCPA guidelines
6️⃣ Charged anyway? Ask for it to be removed, or call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915

Source: CCPA Guidelines on service charge, 4 July 2022; Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sec 18(2)(l))
