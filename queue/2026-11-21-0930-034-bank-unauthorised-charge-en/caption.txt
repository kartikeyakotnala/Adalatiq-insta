Your bank charged you for something you didn't agree to? Here's where to complain. 👇

1️⃣ First, complain to your bank
2️⃣ No reply in 30 days, or not satisfied? Go to the RBI Ombudsman
3️⃣ File within 90 days after that, on cms.rbi.org.in or at crpc@rbi.org.in
4️⃣ It's free. There is no fee to file or resolve a complaint
5️⃣ The Ombudsman can award up to Rs 30 lakh for consequential loss, plus up to Rs 3 lakh for time, expenses and harassment
6️⃣ The new RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 is in force from 1 July 2026
