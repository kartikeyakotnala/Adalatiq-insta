Renting a home on a handshake? Here's when the law needs it in writing. 👇

1️⃣ A lease for more than one year, year to year, or with a yearly rent can be made only by a registered instrument
2️⃣ Other leases can be oral, if possession is handed over, or by a registered instrument (your State's rent or registration law may add rules)
3️⃣ A registered lease must be signed by both landlord and tenant
4️⃣ A lease that had to be registered but wasn't can't affect the property or be used as evidence of the deal
5️⃣ Stamp duty and rent-control rules depend on your State
6️⃣ The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a model for States, not a law everywhere

Source: Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Sec 105, 107); Registration Act, 1908 (Sec 17, 49); Constitution, Seventh Schedule; PIB
