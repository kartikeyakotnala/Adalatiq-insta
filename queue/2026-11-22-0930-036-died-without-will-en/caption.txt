Someone in the family died without a will? Here's who inherits. 👇

1️⃣ First check which law applies: Hindus, Buddhists, Jains and Sikhs follow the Hindu Succession Act, 1956; Muslims follow Muslim Personal Law; Christians and Parsis generally follow the Indian Succession Act, 1925. In Uttarakhand, the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 now applies to residents (except Scheduled Tribes)
2️⃣ When a Hindu man dies, Class I heirs inherit first: sons, daughters, widow, mother and others listed
3️⃣ They inherit together; the widow (all widows together), each son, each daughter and the mother get one share each
4️⃣ No Class I heir? Then Class II heirs, then agnates, then cognates
5️⃣ When a Hindu woman dies, her children and husband come first, then her husband's heirs, then her parents

Source: Hindu Succession Act, 1956 (Sec 2, 8, 9, 10, 15, Schedule); Shariat Act, 1937 (Sec 2); Indian Succession Act, 1925 (Sec 29)
