Want your wishes followed after you're gone? Here's how a valid will is made. 👇

1️⃣ Anyone of sound mind who is not a minor can make a will
2️⃣ Sign it (or put your mark), and have at least two witnesses sign in your presence
3️⃣ No legal jargon is needed, and no particular form of attestation
4️⃣ Registering a will is optional, and can be done at any time
5️⃣ A will made through fraud or coercion is void
6️⃣ You can revoke or change it at any time
⚠️ These rules don't apply to wills of Muslims. In Uttarakhand, the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 now governs wills of residents (except Scheduled Tribes)

Source: Indian Succession Act, 1925 (Sec 57, 58, 59, 61, 62, 63, 70, 74); Registration Act, 1908 (Sec 18, 27)
