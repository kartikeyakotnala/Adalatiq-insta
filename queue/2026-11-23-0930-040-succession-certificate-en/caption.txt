Need to collect a deceased relative's dues, shares or debentures? Here's what a succession certificate does. 👇

1️⃣ A District Judge grants it for the debts and securities you list, like money owed to the deceased, government securities, and company shares or debentures
2️⃣ A court won't make the deceased's debtor pay you without a succession certificate, probate or letters of administration
3️⃣ Anyone who pays the certificate holder in good faith is fully protected
4️⃣ Apply to the District Judge where the deceased ordinarily lived; the court fee depends on your State
5️⃣ The Judge decides summarily, may ask for a bond, and an appeal from the District Judge lies to the High Court
⚠️ It doesn't finally settle who owns the property. In Uttarakhand, the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 governs succession for residents (except Scheduled Tribes), so check how it applies

Source: Indian Succession Act, 1925 (Sec 2, 214, 370–375, 379, 381, 384, 387)
