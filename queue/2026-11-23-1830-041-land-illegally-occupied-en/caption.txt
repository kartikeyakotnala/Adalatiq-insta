Someone has taken over your land? Here's what the law lets you do. 👇

1️⃣ Thrown out without consent and without due process? Sue to get possession back within 6 months of dispossession
2️⃣ That quick suit can't be filed against the Government, and there's no appeal, but you can still sue on title later
3️⃣ A suit for possession based on title generally has 12 years
4️⃣ Once the limitation period ends, your right to the property is extinguished, so don't wait
5️⃣ Criminal trespass is punishable with up to 3 months' jail, a ₹5,000 fine, or both (up to 1 year for a home)
6️⃣ You can give the information to the police station's officer in charge
⚠️ Check your State's laws too: Gujarat, for example, has a Land Grabbing (Prohibition) Act, 2020

Source: Specific Relief Act, 1963 (Sec 5, 6); Limitation Act, 1963 (Sec 27, Art 64, 65); Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 329); BNSS, 2023 (Sec 173)
