Just married and wondering about registration? Here's how it works. 👇

1️⃣ Hindu marriages: each State makes its own registration rules, so the procedure depends on your State
2️⃣ A State can make registration compulsory, but not registering does not make a Hindu marriage invalid
3️⃣ The marriage register is admissible as evidence, so registering gives you proof
4️⃣ Special Marriage Act: give written notice to the Marriage Officer of a district where one of you lived 30+ days
5️⃣ Objections can be made within 30 days; then you and three witnesses sign, and a certificate is entered
⚠️ Uttarakhand (Scheduled Tribes excluded): under the State's Uniform Civil Code, a marriage must be submitted for registration within 60 days

Source: Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 (Sec 8); Special Marriage Act, 1954 (Sec 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16); Uniform Civil Code Rules, Uttarakhand, 2025 (Rule 8)
