Both of you want to end the marriage? Here's how mutual consent divorce works. 👇

1️⃣ You must have lived separately for one year or more, and both agree the marriage should end
2️⃣ File one joint petition in the district court
3️⃣ Make a second joint motion 6 to 18 months later, if the petition isn't withdrawn
4️⃣ The court must be satisfied your consent wasn't obtained by force, fraud or undue influence
5️⃣ The Supreme Court has held the 6-month wait can be waived by the court in suitable cases
6️⃣ The Special Marriage Act has the same one-year and 6–18 month timeline
⚠️ In Uttarakhand, the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 now applies to residents (except Scheduled Tribes)

Source: Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 (Sec 13B, 19, 23); Special Marriage Act, 1954 (Sec 28); Supreme Court, Shilpa Sailesh v. Varun Sreenivasan (2023)
