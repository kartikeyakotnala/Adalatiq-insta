Husband not supporting you? The law gives a wife a right to maintenance. 👇

1️⃣ If a husband with sufficient means neglects or refuses to maintain a wife who can't maintain herself, a Magistrate can order a monthly allowance (BNSS Sec 144)
2️⃣ A divorced wife who hasn't remarried can claim too
3️⃣ Interim maintenance and case expenses can be ordered while the case is pending
4️⃣ If he doesn't pay, he can be jailed for up to a month for each unpaid month; apply within one year
5️⃣ Hindu wives can also claim under the Hindu Marriage Act and Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act; DV Act victims can get monetary relief
6️⃣ Women are entitled to free legal aid from Legal Services Authorities
⚠️ In Uttarakhand, the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 now governs marriage and divorce of residents (except Scheduled Tribes)

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 144, 145); Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 (Sec 24, 25); Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956 (Sec 18); Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (Sec 20); Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Sec 12)
