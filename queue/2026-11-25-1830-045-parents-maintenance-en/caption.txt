Children refusing to support their elderly parents? The law is on the parents' side. 👇

1️⃣ Parents and grandparents who can't maintain themselves can claim maintenance from adult children and grandchildren
2️⃣ Maintenance covers food, clothing, residence and medical treatment
3️⃣ Apply to the Maintenance Tribunal; it should decide within 90 days of notice, and can order interim maintenance meanwhile
4️⃣ The State sets the maximum, which cannot exceed ₹10,000 a month
5️⃣ Property given on condition of care can be declared void if the care stops
6️⃣ Abandoning a senior citizen in your care can mean up to 3 months' jail, a ₹5,000 fine, or both
⚠️ Parents can also claim under BNSS Sec 144, but not under both laws. Appeal against a Tribunal order within 60 days

Source: Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007 (Sec 2, 4, 5, 9, 12, 16, 23, 24); Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 144)
