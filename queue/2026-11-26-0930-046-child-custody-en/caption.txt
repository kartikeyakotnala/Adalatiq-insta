Divorce and children: who decides custody, and how? 👇

1️⃣ The child's welfare comes first; for Hindu children it is the paramount consideration
2️⃣ The court looks at the child's age, sex and religion, and each guardian's character, capacity and relationship with the child
3️⃣ If the child is old enough to form an intelligent preference, the court may consider it
4️⃣ For a Hindu child under five, custody shall ordinarily be with the mother
5️⃣ Custody orders in Hindu Marriage Act cases can later be revoked, suspended or varied
6️⃣ Custody and visiting-rights cases go to the Family Court, where one exists
⚠️ In Uttarakhand, the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 now governs marriage and divorce of residents (except Scheduled Tribes)

Source: Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 (Sec 17); Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act, 1956 (Sec 6, 13); Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 (Sec 26); Family Courts Act, 1984 (Sec 7, 8)
