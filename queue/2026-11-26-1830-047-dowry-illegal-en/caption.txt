"We gave it willingly" is not a defence. Here's what the law says about dowry. 👇

1️⃣ Giving, taking or helping with dowry is a crime: at least 5 years in jail and a fine
2️⃣ Dowry means property or valuables given in connection with the marriage, before, at or after it
3️⃣ Even demanding dowry is punishable with up to 2 years in jail
4️⃣ Gifts given at the wedding without any demand are exempt only if listed; gifts from the bride's side must also be customary and not excessive
5️⃣ Any dowry received by others must be transferred to the woman
6️⃣ Complain to the police or a Magistrate; dowry cruelty and dowry death are offences under the BNS

Source: Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 (Sec 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 8A, 8B); Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Sec 80, 85, 86)
