Abuse at home isn't only about being hit. Here's what the law covers and how to get protection. 👇

1️⃣ Domestic violence includes physical, sexual, verbal, emotional and economic abuse
2️⃣ It protects women in a shared household: wives, women in a relationship in the nature of marriage, and family members
3️⃣ Contact your district's Protection Officer or the police, and apply to the Magistrate
4️⃣ The first hearing is ordinarily within 3 days
5️⃣ The Magistrate can order protection, residence, money, custody and compensation
6️⃣ Breaking a protection order can mean up to 1 year in jail. In danger? Call 181 or 112

Source: Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (Sec 2, 3, 12, 17–23, 31, 32)
