Single and want to adopt? The law allows it. Here's how. 👇

1️⃣ A single or divorced person can adopt if they meet the eligibility criteria
2️⃣ Through CARA, a single woman can adopt a child of any gender; a single man cannot adopt a girl child
3️⃣ Age limits for singles range from 40 (child up to 2 years) to 55 (child above 8)
4️⃣ There must be at least a 25-year age gap between you and the child
5️⃣ Register on CARINGS, CARA's online portal; a Home Study Report follows
6️⃣ The District Magistrate issues the adoption order

Source: Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015 (Sec 57, 61); Adoption Regulations, 2022 (Reg 5)
