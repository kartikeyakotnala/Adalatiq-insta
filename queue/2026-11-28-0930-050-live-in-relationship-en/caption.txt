Living together without marriage? Here's what the law says. 👇

1️⃣ The Domestic Violence Act, 2005 covers a "relationship in the nature of marriage" between partners who share a household
2️⃣ The Supreme Court has said not every live-in relationship qualifies
3️⃣ It looks at whether you present yourselves as like spouses, are of legal age, are otherwise qualified to marry (incl. unmarried), and lived together voluntarily for a significant time
4️⃣ A woman in such a relationship who faces domestic violence can seek protection, residence, monetary relief and compensation from the Magistrate
⚠️ In Uttarakhand, live-in couples must register with the Registrar under the State's Uniform Civil Code, 2024 (Scheduled Tribes excluded)

Source: Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (Sec 2, 12, 18, 19, 20, 22); Supreme Court, D. Velusamy v. D. Patchaiammal (2010); Uniform Civil Code Rules, Uttarakhand, 2025 (Rule 15)
