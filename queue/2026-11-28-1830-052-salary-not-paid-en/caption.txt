Salary not paid on time? Here's what the law says and what you can do. 👇

1️⃣ Monthly wages must be paid before the 7th day of the next month ends
2️⃣ Removed, dismissed, retrenched or resigned? Wages are due within two working days
3️⃣ Total deductions cannot cross 50% of your wages
4️⃣ You, your registered union or the Inspector-cum-Facilitator can file a claim before the authority under the Code; which office handles it depends on whether the Centre or your State is the appropriate Government
5️⃣ File within three years; a late claim may be heard for sufficient cause
6️⃣ The authority can order compensation up to 10 times the claim
⚠️ The Code on Wages, 2019 is in force from 21 Nov 2025 and replaced the Payment of Wages Act, 1936

Source: Code on Wages, 2019 (Sec 17, 18, 45, 49, 69)
