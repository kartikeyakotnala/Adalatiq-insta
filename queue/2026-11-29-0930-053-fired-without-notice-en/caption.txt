Lost your job without notice? Here's what the Industrial Relations Code says. 👇

1️⃣ These rules protect a 'worker'. Staff mainly in managerial or administrative roles, and supervisors earning over ₹18,000 a month, are not 'workers'
2️⃣ A worker with one year's continuous service can't be retrenched without one month's written notice with reasons, or a month's wages instead
3️⃣ Retrenchment compensation is 15 days' average pay for every completed year of service
4️⃣ Dismissal as a disciplinary punishment, voluntary retirement, superannuation and end of a fixed-term contract are not 'retrenchment'
5️⃣ A factory, mine or plantation with 300+ workers needs three months' notice and prior government permission
6️⃣ To challenge a termination, apply to the conciliation officer; after 45 days you can go to the Tribunal, within two years
⚠️ The IR Code is in force from 21 Nov 2025 and replaced the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947

Source: Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Sec 2, 4, 70, 77, 79, 83, 104)
