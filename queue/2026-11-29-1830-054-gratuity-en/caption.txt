Leaving your job after years of service? Here's how gratuity works. 👇

1️⃣ Covers shops and establishments with 10+ employees, plus factories, mines, plantations, ports and railway companies
2️⃣ Usually needs 5 years' continuous service; not needed on death or disablement
3️⃣ Fixed-term employees become eligible after 1 year
4️⃣ Formula: 15 days' wages (monthly wages ÷ 26 × 15) for every completed year, or part over six months
5️⃣ Maximum is currently ₹20 lakh, as notified by the Centre
6️⃣ Employer must pay within 30 days of it becoming payable, or pay simple interest
⚠️ The Code on Social Security, 2020 is in force from 21 Nov 2025 and replaced the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972

Source: Code on Social Security, 2020 (Sec 53, 56, First Schedule); Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Sec 2(o))
