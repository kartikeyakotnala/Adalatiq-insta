# Fact-check: 003 Can the police arrest me without a warrant? (en, hi)

Source: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 – ss. 35, 47, 48 (https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20099/3/a2023-46.pdf)

**Pass 1: FAIL.** Slide 2 and caption line 2 said arrest "must be necessary" for all offences up to 7 years. The necessity
test in s.35(1)(b) covers only a person accused on complaint/information/suspicion, not s.35(1)(a) (offence committed
in front of police). Also: s.35(7) is "DSP or above"; refs tightened to 47(1), 47(2), 48(1); several Hindi wording fixes.

**Pass 2 (after fixes): PASS.** All claims verified against s.35(1)(a), 35(1)(b)(i)-(ii)(a)-(e), 35(3), 35(5), 35(6), 35(7),
47(1), 47(2), 48(1). Hindi matches the English and the law.
