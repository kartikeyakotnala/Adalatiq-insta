# Fact-check: 005 Can a shop charge more than the MRP? (en, hi)

Sources: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (Rules 2(m), 3, 6(1)(e), 18(2) in the 2011 text, via
legalmetrology.nagaland.gov.in); PIB 8 Nov 2021 on the 2021 amendment ("Mandatory declaration of MRP in Indian currency
inclusive of all taxes"); National Consumer Helpline (consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/about).

**Pass 1: FAIL.** Slide 4 said MRP must be on "every package"; Rule 3 exempts packs over 25 kg/25 L and goods for
industrial/institutional buyers. Clause letters (2(m), 6(1)(e), 18(2)) could not be confirmed against post-2011 amendments.
**Pass 2: FAIL (one fix).** Slide 4 ref needed the PIB release, which is the source for "in rupees".
**Pass 3: PASS** after fixes: refs cite rule numbers without sub-clause letters, slide 4 scoped to retail packs with exemptions,
PIB added. Residual note: whether Rule 3's exemptions changed after 2021 could not be confirmed from official sources.
