# Fact-check: 007 Do daughters have a share in joint family property? (en)

Sources: Hindu Succession Act, 1956 as amended 2005 (ss. 2(1), 2(2), 6(1) and proviso, 6(3)(a), 6(5) Explanation, 8,
10 Rule 2, 30, Schedule Class I) via Govt of Tamil Nadu CRA copy; Supreme Court, Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma,
(2020) 10 SCR 135, para 129 (AP High Court copy).

**Pass 1: FAIL.** "Ancestral property" was overbroad (s.6 covers coparcenary property of a Mitakshara joint family;
a father's self-acquired property is not a birthright). Slide 5's "only registered deed or decree" was wrong (SC accepts
oral partitions in exceptional cases backed by public documents). Slide 4 lacked the "died on or after 9.9.2005"
condition; slide 6 lacked the Scheduled Tribes exclusion (s.2(2)) and s.2(1)(c).
**Pass 2: FAIL (citations).** New caption line on self-acquired property needed ss. 8, 10, 30 cited.
**Pass 3: PASS** after all fixes (citation fix verified by the checker's pass-2 quotes; no claim changed after it).
