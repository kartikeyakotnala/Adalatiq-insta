## Dropped
- **"Can police take my phone and check it?"** Drafter recommended DROP: BNSS ss.94/105/106 cover producing and seizing a
  device, but whether police can make you unlock it or search it turns on Art 20(3)/privacy case law that official text
  doesn't settle. A "they can take it" post alone could mislead. Left unticked in topics.md with a note. The reserve
  topic (court case status) took its place.

## Worth a closer look
- **Sourcing of later BNSS sections.** The fetch tool could not read BNSS past ~s.130 directly. s.179 (014) rests on MHA's
  verbatim reproduction of the proviso (PIB-hosted); s.187 (011) on a Delhi High Court judgment that reproduces it;
  s.478/480 (012) on the Uttarakhand Judicial & Legal Academy handbook; s.482 (013) on a Supreme Court judgment that
  quotes it; s.173 (015) on PIB/MHA documents. All are official, but not the bare Act PDF.
- **016 cyber fraud:** RBI's customer-liability rules change for transactions from **1 Jan 2027** (new Section DA:
  5 calendar days, compensation up to ₹25,000). The post deliberately avoids day counts; consider a follow-up post in January.
- **018 private photos:** platforms' takedown deadline is now **2 hours** (IT Rules amendment of 10 Feb 2026), not 24.
- **019 Good Samaritan:** MV Act s.134A and GSR 594(E) were read via PIB (MoRTH) releases, not the gazette text.
- **017 online threats:** whether criminal intimidation is cognizable wasn't confirmed, so the post uses the law's own
  "cognizable offence" wording for the e-complaint slide.
- **State-dependent:** 015 says online FIR options depend on your State. Maharashtra has a 2026 BNSS amendment Bill
  (ss.15, 107, 129, 170, 173(3)) — it doesn't touch any section used here.
